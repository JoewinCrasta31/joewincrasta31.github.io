About

I make images and video move people — then figure out what to do next with the data behind that.

I started on the numbers side of things — a Bachelor of Commerce, then a run in clerical and accounting work at a financial cooperative in India — before crossing over into multimedia. That background still shapes how I work: structured, deadline-literate, and comfortable owning a project end to end.

Today I'm a Multimedia Executive at Babygrow FZC in Sharjah's Al Hamriyah Free Zone, working across photography, videography, AI-assisted video production, and post-production.
